Graph Machine Learning

Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP paris) · Feb 2026

This course introduces the fundamental concepts and techniques of Graph Machine Learning, including Graph Signal Processing (GSP), Graph Learning, and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). With a particular focus on biomedical applications, students will explore how graph-based methods can model, analyze, and learn from complex biological and medical data, including EEG signals, brain networks, and medical imaging. The course combines theoretical foundations with practical applications to address real-world challenges in biomedical data analysis.

Level

MSc

Master 2 Biomedical Engineering (BME) track

Prerequisites

Linear algebra, probability, Basic ML

Basic Python, vectors and matrices, probability, and introductory machine learning.

Format

6 sessions (tutorials + lab) (24 hours)

Course outcomes

Learning objectives

By the end of this lecture, students will be able to:

01

Understand the fundamental concepts of graph-based representations and Graph Signal Processing (GSP) for modeling and analyzing complex biomedical data.

02

Apply Graph Learning techniques to construct and analyze graphs from biomedical signals and data, including EEG signals and brain networks.

03

Explore Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and their applications in biomedical data analysis, including disease classification and brain connectivity analysis.

04

Develop and evaluate graph-based Machine Learning models to address real-world challenges in biomedical signal processing and medical imaging.
